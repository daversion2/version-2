# Neuro-Nudge — Brand

*Worked out with the founder 2026-11-27, through a series of one-question
interviews built on the market research in
`reports/Neuro Nudge go to market messaging.md`. This is the source of truth
for how Neuro-Nudge talks, in the app, on the site, in ads and on merch. Copy
marked "(draft)" hasn't been tested on anyone.*

Neuro-Nudge is an app **and** a brand: it's meant to be something people
identify with and wear, not only download. The money side (subscription,
merch, anything else) is deliberately not decided here.

---

## The line

> **Do hard shit most of the time.**

The swear isn't the point; **"most of the time" is**. The first half is tough,
the second half is reasonable, and that turn is the whole brand. "Do hard
things" was rejected: it's a book title and a gym-wall cliché, and it has no
second half.

The App Store and ad platforms will need a clean version. Not chosen yet.
Options from the session: "Usually the hard way.", "The hard way, most of the
time.", "Do hard sh*t most of the time." (asterisk as part of the joke).

## The enemy: Comfort Mode

> **Comfort Mode is on by default.** Your phone, your feed, your fridge and
> your couch switched it on. You left it on.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where it comes from | **Both.** Products turned the difficulty down, and we let them. |
| How we treat it | **Expose it, calmly.** Show how Comfort Mode gets switched on (autoplay, one-tap delivery, food built so you can't stop). Explain; never lecture. |
| What it should make people think | **"I've been coasting."** Honest recognition, no shame. Not "I've been played" (blames only the companies), not guilt. |
| Shape | A **condition** you drift into, not a villain character. |

Comfort is not the enemy. Comfort you never chose is. Eat the cake; just know
Comfort Mode served it.

Rejected names, so nobody re-proposes them: engineered easy (cold, too
intellectual), autopilot (no character), the lazy river (founder disliked),
Easy Mode (close second), Big Comfort (close, not quite). "The soft life" is
off-limits: it's a positive self-care movement for many people, especially
Black women.

**Comfort Inc.** — a fictional company that "switched Comfort Mode on" — is
parked. Revisit when content starts (parody ads, "Comfort Inc. presents:
autoplay"). Not used in the core message until then.

**Before merch:** "Comfort mode" is a common phrase (car drive modes, comfort
and sport). That helps recognition and suggests a visual (a drive-mode dial),
but run a trademark check before anything goes to print.

## The move: override

> **Override Comfort Mode.**

"Override" is already the app's word. Every "how hard was it to start?" rating
is a logged override, so the brand and the product say the same thing. An
override is momentary and repeatable, which fits "most of the time": you don't
switch Comfort Mode off forever, you override it again today.

## Who our people are

> **Neuro-Nudge people are capable.** Not perfect, not preachy, not in it for
> the pain. They do hard shit most of the time, because small hard things are
> practice for the big ones life will throw at them. They're openly proud of
> the effort. They don't lecture anyone still in Comfort Mode; they show what
> the other way looks like, and explain if you ask.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Core trait | **Capable.** Not fragile; discomfort doesn't run them. |
| The payoff | **Ready for life.** The hard day you can't schedule (illness, loss, a big chance) is what the small overrides are practice for. |
| Never seen as | **Preachy, perfect, masochistic.** *Try-hard was not ruled out*: open pride in effort is fine. |
| Toward people still in Comfort Mode | **Lead by example.** Say nothing; be visibly capable; explain if asked. |
| What a shirt is for | **Signalling to others.** A person who never preaches lets the shirt do the talking. |
| Group name | **None. Let it emerge.** Watch what pod members call themselves once Pods launches; adopt the one that sticks. |

The audience is **anyone who worries modern life is making them soft**, not
only the Huberman/Goggins crowd. Use "soft" with a wink ("modern life is making
us soft, and honestly the couch is very nice"), never as a sneer at other
people. It's the alpha crowd's favourite word; we use it about ourselves.

## Voice

**Calm, self-aware, funny. Never alpha, never preachy.**

| Choice | Answer | Looks like (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| Humour | **Self-aware.** The joke is usually on us, including that we're an app on your phone telling you to use your phone less. | "You found us on Instagram. Good. Now close it." |
| Swearing | **Mild, anywhere it sounds natural** — shit, damn, hell. Sparingly. Nothing stronger. | "Hard to start. Did it anyway. Damn." |
| Speaker | **"We", the brand.** | "We built this because we kept skipping workouts too." |
| Science | **Depends where.** Ads and social: one plain idea, no jargon. In-app "Why this works" and the website: go deeper, name studies. | Ad: "Your brain predicts the cost. You feel it as resistance." Site: "Gardner (2016): it's the starting that becomes automatic." |
| Bad days | **A self-aware joke, then move on.** No guilt, no pep talk. | "Comfort Mode got you. It gets us too. Back at it?" |
| Wins | **Understated.** A nod, not a parade. Respect through restraint. | "That was a 3. Noted." |
| Rhythm | **Mix.** Short lines for headlines, buttons and moments; full, conversational sentences for explanations, emails and the origin story. | Moment: "Hard to start. Did it anyway." Explanation: "The first two minutes are the worst part, and you just got through them." |

### Do / don't

| We sound like | We never sound like |
|---|---|
| A friend who lifts and still orders pizza | A drill sergeant |
| "Comfort Mode got you. It gets us too." | "No excuses." |
| "That was a 3. Noted." | "LEGENDARY! 🔥 You're UNSTOPPABLE!" |
| "Yes, we see the irony." | Holier-than-thou about screens or food |
| "Your brain predicts the cost." | "Boost your dopamine" / "grow your aMCC" |
| "Most of the time." | "Every single day." / "Never miss." |
| Showing how a feed keeps you | Telling people what they should eat |

### Words

- **Ours:** Comfort Mode, override, hard start, "a 3", most of the time,
  capable, noted.
- **Avoid:** alpha, beast, crush, grind, savage, warrior, "no excuses",
  "never miss", dopamine, "unlock your potential", "1% better", "practice"
  and "rep" (existing app rule, see `docs/app-overview-user-facing.md`).

### Science guardrails

The voice can be confident; the claims can't outrun the evidence. From the
research report:
- Safe: "your brain predicts the cost and you feel it as resistance";
  "starting is the part that becomes automatic"; "research links the aMCC to
  persistence"; "rewarding effort makes people choose harder things".
- Not safe: anything saying the app changes or grows your brain; "willpower is
  a muscle"; "it's always easier than you think"; dopamine or health claims
  for cold exposure; "you're getting stronger" as a measured fact. Say "see
  how hard your hard things feel to you, over time."

## The brand in one breath

> **Do hard shit most of the time.** Comfort Mode is on by default: your
> phone, your feed and your couch switched it on, and you left it on. Override
> it. Not to suffer, but because small hard things are how you get ready for
> the big ones. Neuro-Nudge counts every override, and the hard ones count
> more.

Lines in the voice (drafts): "Comfort Mode is on by default. Override it." /
"Train for the hard day you can't schedule." / "Every override counts. Hard
ones count more." / "Don't preach. Just override." / Merch: "Comfort Mode: Off
(mostly)", "Rated a 3. Overrode it anyway."

## Where the app doesn't match yet

Found while writing this; nothing changed yet.
- **Streak tiers are hype:** "On Fire", "Unstoppable", "Legendary"
  (`src/constants/willpower.ts`), shown by the celebration modal. Wins are
  meant to be understated.
- **No "Comfort Mode" anywhere** in the app, site or onboarding yet. The
  landing page (`hosting/index.html`) uses "the 'I'll start tomorrow' voice"
  for the same idea.
- **Landing-page science lines** flagged in the research report ("strengthening
  the part of your brain…", "the effort region works hardest") break the
  guardrails above.
- **Mild swearing in the app** would likely move the App Store age rating
  (Apple's "infrequent/mild profanity" setting). Check before shipping any.
