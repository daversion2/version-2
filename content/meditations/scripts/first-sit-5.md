# Opening — settle in. Short sentences: each paragraph is voiced as one piece.

Welcome. This is a five-minute sit, and there's nothing to get right.

Find a way of sitting that's steady, but not stiff. Feet on the floor if you're in a chair. Hands wherever they rest easily.

[pause 3s]

Let your eyes close, or rest them on a spot on the floor a little way in front of you.

[pause 4s]

Take one slower breath in through your nose...

[pause 2s]

and let it go.

[pause 4s]

Now let your breathing do whatever it does on its own. You're not controlling it. You're just going to watch it.

[pause 5s]

Pick one place where you feel the breath most clearly. Maybe the cool air at the tip of your nose. Maybe your chest, or your belly, rising and falling.

[pause 5s]

Rest your attention there.

[pause 12s]

# The whole skill in one idea: wandering isn't failure, noticing is the point.

Before long, your mind will wander. A plan, a memory, an itch, the urge to check the time. That isn't a mistake. It's what minds do.

The moment you notice you've drifted is the moment that counts. Notice where you went, and bring your attention back to the breath. No need to scold yourself. Just come back.

[pause 20s]

# FOR REVIEW — the one science-flavoured line. Keep it, soften it, or cut it?

Each time you come back, you're training the part of your brain that chooses where attention goes. It's the same skill you use to stay with something hard, instead of reaching for an escape.

[pause 20s]

If it helps, you can quietly label what pulled you away. Thinking. Planning. Hearing. Then back to the breath.

[pause 25s]

You might notice a pull to open your eyes, or to check how long is left. See if you can let that urge be there without acting on it. It rises, and it passes.

[pause 25s]

Back to the breath, wherever you feel it.

[pause 20s]

# Closing

We're coming to the end.

Let go of watching the breath, and take in the whole body sitting here. The weight of it. The sounds in the room.

[pause 6s]

Take one fuller breath in...

[pause 2s]

and out.

[pause 3s]

When you're ready, open your eyes. Notice how you feel right now, without needing it to be anything in particular.

[pause 2s]

[bell]
