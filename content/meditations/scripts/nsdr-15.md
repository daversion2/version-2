# NSDR, 15 minutes, mostly body scan (founder, 2026-11-01).
# Shape: settle with long exhales (~1.5 min) → slow scan, feet to head, one
# side then the other where it helps (~10 min) → a short rest in the whole
# body (~2 min) → an easy return, or permission to sleep (~1 min).
# Pauses are relative: length_min fits the total to 15:00.
# Claims stay modest: rest, a slower body, a quieter mind. No "resets your
# dopamine" or other things the research doesn't support.

# --- SETTLE --------------------------------------------------------------------

Welcome. For the next little while, there's nothing to do and nowhere to get to. You're just going to rest, while staying awake enough to follow along.

Lie down on your back if you can, arms by your sides, palms facing up or down, whichever feels easier. If you're sitting, let the chair hold all of your weight.

[pause 4s]

Let your eyes close.

[pause 4s]

Take a breath in through your nose, and then a longer breath out through your mouth, like a slow sigh.

[pause 4s]

Once more. In through the nose...

[pause 2s]

and a long, slow breath out.

[pause 5s]

One more time, and let the out-breath be the longest yet.

[pause 6s]

Now let the breath go back to doing whatever it does on its own. You don't need to manage it.

[pause 6s]

Feel the places where your body touches the floor, or the bed, or the chair. The back of your head. Your shoulders. Your back. Your heels. Let each of them get a little heavier.

[pause 10s]

If your mind is busy, that's fine. You're not trying to empty it. Each time you notice you've drifted, just come back to the part of the body we're on.

[pause 8s]

# --- BODY SCAN: feet to head ----------------------------------------------------

Bring your attention down to your feet.

[pause 4s]

Start with the toes of your left foot. Notice whatever is there. Warmth, coolness, tingling, the touch of a sock or the air. Or maybe nothing much at all. Whatever you find is fine.

[pause 12s]

Now the sole of your left foot. The heel. The top of the foot. Let the whole foot soften.

[pause 12s]

Move across to your right foot. The toes.

[pause 8s]

The sole. The heel. The top of the foot. Let it soften, the same way.

[pause 12s]

Now both feet together, resting and heavy.

[pause 10s]

Let your attention move up into your ankles, and then your lower legs. The calves. The shins. If there's any tightness, you don't need to fix it. Just notice it, and let the breath move past it.

[pause 15s]

Your knees. The front of the knees, and the backs of them.

[pause 10s]

Your thighs. The big muscles at the front, and underneath, where the legs rest on the floor. Let them be completely supported.

[pause 15s]

Now your hips, and the bowl of the pelvis. Let this whole area grow heavy and settle down.

[pause 15s]

Bring your attention to your lower back. Notice where it touches the floor, and where it doesn't. Let it soften a little more with each out-breath.

[pause 15s]

Move up through the middle of your back, and then your upper back, between the shoulder blades.

[pause 15s]

Now the belly. Notice it rising as you breathe in, and falling as you breathe out. You don't need to change anything. Just feel it move.

[pause 15s]

Your chest. The ribs widening a little on the in-breath, and settling on the out-breath.

[pause 15s]

Bring your attention to your hands. The fingertips of both hands at once. Then the palms. The backs of the hands.

[pause 12s]

Your wrists. Your forearms. Your elbows.

[pause 10s]

Your upper arms, and up into the shoulders. Shoulders often hold on without us noticing. See if yours can drop, even a little, away from your ears.

[pause 15s]

Now your neck. The front of the throat, the sides, and the back of the neck where it meets the head. Let it be loose.

[pause 15s]

Your jaw. Let your teeth part slightly, and let your tongue rest wherever it falls.

[pause 10s]

Your cheeks. The muscles around your mouth. Let your face be soft, with no expression at all.

[pause 10s]

Your eyes. Let them be heavy in their sockets. The small muscles around them, letting go.

[pause 12s]

Your forehead. Let it smooth out and widen.

[pause 10s]

And the top of your head, and the back of it, resting its full weight on the floor.

[pause 15s]

# --- REST: the whole body ------------------------------------------------------

Now feel your whole body at once, from the top of your head to the soles of your feet. One body, resting.

[pause 15s]

There's nothing left to scan, and nothing to do. Let your body stay exactly where it is, heavy and still, and let your mind rest with it.

[pause 40s]

If you've drifted off into thought, gently come back to the feeling of the whole body, breathing.

[pause 40s]

# --- RETURN --------------------------------------------------------------------

In a moment, we'll finish. If you're using this to fall asleep, you can stay right where you are and let yourself drift. You don't need to come back.

[pause 6s]

Otherwise, start to let a little movement return. Wiggle your fingers and toes.

[pause 6s]

Take a fuller breath in, and stretch in any way that feels good.

[pause 6s]

When you're ready, let your eyes open, and take your time getting up.

[pause 4s]

[bell]
