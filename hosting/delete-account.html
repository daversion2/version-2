Neuro-Nudge

Delete your account

Neuro-Nudge (com.neuronudge.neuronudge) lets you delete your account and all of your data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

How to request deletion

1 From inside the app

This is the fastest route — deletion happens immediately.

  1. Open Neuro-Nudge and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice. Your data is erased and you are signed out.

2 By email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email jonny@neuro-nudge.net with the subject “Delete my Neuro-Nudge account.”

Send it from the email address on the account so we can confirm the request is yours. We will not ask you for your password. Requests are completed within 30 days, and usually within a few business days.

Deletion is permanent. Your account cannot be restored and none of your history can be recovered. If you only want a fresh start while keeping your login, use Settings → Clear Account in the app instead.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is removed:

  • Your sign-in credentials — email and password, or the link to your Google account
  • Profile details: username, email address, and timezone
  • Challenges, habits, completion logs, streaks, and XP
  • Reflections, worksheets, tomorrow plans, and goals
  • Program enrollments and badges
  • Your push-notification token, which stops all reminders

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing that identifies you is kept once deletion completes. Your records are removed from our live database right away.

Residual copies may briefly remain in the routine system backups kept by our hosting provider, Google Firebase, before those backups are overwritten on their normal cycle. They are not used for any purpose and are not accessible to us in normal operation.

We do not sell your data and we retain nothing for advertising or marketing after your account is deleted.

Questions about this process? Email jonny@neuro-nudge.net.
