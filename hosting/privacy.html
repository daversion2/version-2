Neuro-Nudge

Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026. This explains what the Neuro-Nudge app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to get it deleted.

The short version
  • We collect what the app needs to track your habits, and nothing else.
  • No ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and we never sell your data.
  • Your habit history and reflections are visible only to your account.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address, and either a password or a link to your Google account. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • A username, if you choose to set one, and your device's timezone (so reminders and daily totals land on the right day).

What you log in the app

  • The habits you add, their schedules, reminders and action plans.
  • Each check-in: the date, how hard it was to start, and anything you choose to record with it, such as notes, minutes, steps or other amounts, and what helped you get started.
  • Your nightly reflections: how hard you pushed yourself and your plan for the next day.
  • Reasons you give for a skipped week, and cravings you log in Craving Crusher.

Some of this can say something about your health, habits or mood. We treat all of it as private.

Notifications

  • If you turn on notifications, a push token that lets us send them to your device. Turning notifications off in the app removes it.
  • Habit reminders are scheduled on your device itself.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, Apple Health data or advertising identifiers. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

How we use it

  • To run the app: saving your habits and history, working out your progress, streaks and friction scores, and drafting tomorrow's plan.
  • To send the notifications you've opted into.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to keep the service secure and working.

We don't use your data for advertising, don't build profiles of you for anyone else, and don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions and Hosting) stores your account and everything you log.
  • Expo delivers push notifications (it receives your push token and the message) and delivers app updates.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it to sign in.
  • Google Forms receives anything you send through “Send Feedback”.

We don't sell or rent personal data. We would only disclose it if the law required us to.

Neuro-Nudge is run by a very small team. The operator can technically reach stored data through the Firebase console, and only does so to support you or fix a problem you've reported.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, primarily in the United States. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, and database security rules allow each account to read and write only its own data.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our live database straight away. See Delete your account for how, and for what happens to routine backups.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data,
  • correct something that's wrong,
  • delete your account and data (you can also do this yourself in Settings), or
  • stop processing it.

Email jonny@neuro-nudge.net from the address on your account. We respond within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Neuro-Nudge is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Questions about privacy? Email jonny@neuro-nudge.net.
